# Reward Contract

1. **Conservation.** The cap limits rendered objects, never value. At the cap, merge a new gem into the nearest existing gem; add its value and recompute tier/colour. Remove the origin heuristic. Gems never expire: periodically consolidate old, far, unclaimed gems using the existing spatial grid, not all-pairs scans. Within a run, `floor XP + awarded base gem XP = dropped XP`. Player XP bonuses are separately earned, not resource loss; reset begins a new ledger. Multi-gem rewards preserve their advertised total, including remainders.
2. **Claimed means collected.** Vacuum marks targeted XP and gold `claimed = true`. Claimed resources home with ramping speed, independent of expiry and magnet timers, and cannot be culled, merged away, or returned to a pool before collection. They may receive merged incoming value. Reset/disposal ends the run. Pool reuse clears claim state and cancels old object-owned callbacks.
3. **Vacuum scope.** Vacuum collects XP and gold only. Health, buffs, rosaries, chests, and other vacuums stay tactical ground pickups. One `Player.callout` shows the newly claimed haul, e.g. `+420 XP · 38 gold`; already claimed resources are not counted twice. XP here means base gem value; gold reflects the collection multiplier. A short global attraction timer may remain for the HUD, but expiry cannot unclaim resources.
4. **Magnetic Field.** Rename timed Magnet to Magnetic Field. Its world radius is `max(3 × effective pickup range, 360)`, never viewport-derived. Effective range includes Attractorb and player modifiers. Recompute while active if pickup range changes. It attracts XP and gold only; it does not claim tactical pickups or promise global collection.
5. **Buff layers.** Every timed buff holds at most three `{strength, expiresAt}` layers measured on a combat-time clock. Effective strength is the maximum unexpired strength. A pickup cannot lower current strength or shorten the current strongest layer. A weaker pickup never extends the stronger layer; it adds only its own tail. Prune expired/dominated layers first; when still full, retain the strongest layer (latest expiry breaks strength ties), then retain the strongest remaining layers, with latest expiry breaking ties. Discarding a fourth weaker layer is allowed; extending a stronger expiry is not. Put labels, colours, duration, strength, HUD wording, and pickup/streak/combo/wave/milestone profiles in `src/data/powerUps.js`. All producers and HUD consumers read that table.
6. **Honest attack speed.** Fire rate means attacks per second. +30% attack speed means base cooldown divided by 1.3, not multiplied by 0.7. Streak profiles use the same table and are never weaker than the corresponding pickup. Preserve current damage/speed tuning until measured simulations justify a change.
7. **No wasted health pickup.** Neither health orbs nor health relics are consumed at full HP (or when healing is disallowed). Show only HP actually restored through `Player.callout`. Do not introduce pickup shake or flashes.
8. **Safe rosary.** Cleanse kills carry `cause: 'rosary'` through damage, death, subclass overrides, and deferred death handling. Suppress hostile explosions, splits, and summon-on-death for that cause. Preserve normal kill credit, XP, and gold. Clear death cause on pool reset. Boss immunity remains.
9. **Guaranteed rewards.** Boss/event rewards bypass floor capacity. Evict the oldest non-guaranteed item when full; if all items are guaranteed, temporarily exceed the cap rather than lose a reward. Boss chests first grant a level to an eligible non-maxed weapon, otherwise an eligible passive level, otherwise gold. No chest evolutions. Ordinary pickups keep their existing capacity policy. Event XP/gold showers conserve their promised amounts.
10. **Queued level-ups.** Stay paused with zero simulation time through every queued pick; no timer-driven playing gap. Show progress such as `1 of 3`. After the final selection grant 0.4 seconds of damage immunity without flashing; preserve longer pre-existing immunity. Reset clears queue/progress/immunity state. A hit-stop callback must not advance gameplay during selection.
11. **Truthful HUD.** Magnet pill time is `max(player magnetic layer remaining, experience.areaMagnetTimer, experience.globalMagnetTimer)`, including system-only effects. Other pills show effective strength and remaining duration without implying a weaker tail sustains the stronger layer. Preserve readable non-overlapping callouts/cards/pills at 1280×720 and 390×844.
12. **Proof and measurement.** Deterministic property tests cover at least 500 random buff sequences (both strength/expiry invariants, layer bound, pruning) and random gem spawns/caps/collections (conservation at every step). Preserve the eleven behavior reproductions. Exercise the real browser objects and crowd scene before acceptance. Only after correctness gates pass, add opt-in `debugCommands.getRewardStats()` reporting combat-time buff coverage, collected/expired pickups, wasted healing, base XP dropped/awarded/on-floor/lost, and damage/attack-speed overlap. Run three seeded ten-minute god-mode kiting simulations before tuning; report measured before/after and decline unsupported changes.

## Integration ownership

A owns XP gems/system and gold resource hooks. B owns the power-up table, Player buff/streak/queue helpers and no-flash immunity, CanvasHUD, and KillMilestoneSystem reward-table migration. C owns floor items, Enemy death plumbing and critical-streak table migration, Wraith/Demon death forwarding if needed, and BossSystem reward calls. D owns game glue, pickup/wave producers, queue UI, and DynamicEventSystem reward integration if needed. Tests are separate workstream files; `tests/pickups.test.js` is orchestrator-integrated. Stop and report before crossing ownership. No new pickups, evolution redesign, unrelated refactors, new shake, per-frame shadowBlur, or full-screen gradients.
